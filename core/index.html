Ten days after a lower-back strain, the order matters: stabilise, load, then power. Each 3D figure loops the movement, and the red glow marks the muscles doing the work. Drag a figure sideways to rotate it and see the movement from the front or back.

Pain rule. Stay at 2/10 or lower during an exercise, and no worse the next morning. Stop and see a doctor for pain below the knee, numbness or tingling, leg weakness, night pain that doesn’t ease, or any bladder or bowel change (urgent). Not improving by 2–3 weeks also means a check-up.
