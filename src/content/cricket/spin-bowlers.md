## Status

Stub, with no exercise selection. No spin-specific source was read. The searches for the [fast bowlers](/strength/cricket/fast-bowlers/), [batters](/strength/cricket/batters/), [injury prevention](/strength/cricket/injury-prevention/) and [season planning](/strength/cricket/season-planning/) pages did not surface one, but a dedicated spin search has not been done, so absence of evidence here is not evidence of absence.

What exists that is relevant:

- In Australian first-class cricket, 4% of spin bowlers were out injured at any time against 14% of pace bowlers ([claim](/plyometrics/sports/cricket/evidence/#claim-plyo-cricket-injury-sites), grade C, professionals, over twenty years old). It says how often, not why.
- The [plyometrics cricket pages](/plyometrics/sports/cricket/) cover landing, hops, stopping and hip-led rotation for all roles.
- The season-planning and injury-prevention pages are written from evidence that is mostly about pace bowlers or batters. Read them for the general method, not as spin-specific advice.

Back pain that persists, or pain in the fingers, wrist, forearm or shoulder that builds across sessions, needs a clinician. This page does not diagnose, rehabilitate or decide return to play.
