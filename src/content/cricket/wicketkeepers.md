## Status

Stub, with no exercise selection. No keeper-specific source was found, and a dedicated search has not been done.

What exists that is relevant:

- In Australian first-class cricket, 2% of wicketkeepers were out injured at any time, against 14% of pace bowlers ([claim](/plyometrics/sports/cricket/evidence/#claim-plyo-cricket-injury-sites), grade C, professionals, over twenty years old). It says how often, not why, and does not cover club keepers.
- The [plyometrics cricket pages](/plyometrics/sports/cricket/) cover landing, stopping and hip-led rotation, and the base squat and hinge exercises are on the [strength exercises](/strength/exercises/) pages. Whether keepers need anything different is a research question, not a finding.
- The strength-side [injury prevention](/strength/cricket/injury-prevention/) and [season planning](/strength/cricket/season-planning/) pages give the general method.

Knee pain that builds across sessions, or back pain that persists, needs a clinician. This page does not diagnose, rehabilitate or decide return to play.
