## Status

This track is a **stub**. It lists the evidence found so far and the research questions still open. It does not prescribe exercises, loads or workloads. Role pages link to general principles and exercises instead of restating them.

## How to read the evidence here

- Cricket injury research is mostly observational. It shows associations, not proven causes.
- Workload ratios are contested. Do not treat any single ratio as a rule.
- Youth bowlers need qualified supervision. See the youth consensus statement before programming for under-18s.
