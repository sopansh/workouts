## What the literature says

A widely cited review proposes that exercise-induced muscle growth is mediated by mechanical tension, muscle damage and metabolic stress, and that all three can play a role. That is a mechanistic framework rather than a prescription: it explains why different training styles can all work without telling you which is best.

## Why this matters for the plan

- Programming choices on this site are made from training studies with growth outcomes wherever they exist.
- Where the only available evidence is EMG (muscle electrical activity), the site labels it as such. EMG differences between exercises do not by themselves show differences in growth.

## Limits

The relative importance of each mechanism is not settled. Do not read the existence of a mechanism as a reason to add a technique that targets it (for example, chasing soreness to induce damage).
