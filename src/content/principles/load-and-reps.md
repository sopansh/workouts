## What the evidence shows

When all sets are taken to or near failure, low-load training (up to about 60% of 1RM) and high-load training produce similar muscle growth, while maximal strength favours heavy loads. A network meta-analysis found that all prescriptions beat no training and that all promoted hypertrophy comparably, while heavier loads were best for strength.

## How the plan applies it

- Main compound lifts: 5 to 8 reps.
- Machine and secondary compound lifts: 8 to 12 reps.
- Isolation work: 10 to 20 reps.

This mix is a practical choice. It is not claimed to be uniquely best for growth.

## Limits

The low-load studies had participants train to or near failure. Very high-rep sets are slow and uncomfortable, and heavy compound lifts add joint and spinal stress.
