## The evidence is split

A 2016 meta-analysis concluded that training a muscle twice a week beat once a week when volume was matched. A 2025 meta-regression that separated direct from indirect sets found the effect of frequency on hypertrophy compatible with a negligible effect. A network meta-analysis ranked higher-load, multi-set, twice-weekly training top for hypertrophy.

## What this plan does

The six-day plan trains each major muscle twice, because splitting about 10 to 20 weekly sets over two sessions keeps each session manageable. The four-day alternative keeps twice-weekly exposure with fewer sessions.

## Limits

Most frequency studies compare one to three sessions per week. There is little controlled evidence on more than three exposures per week, and frequency interacts with volume and recovery.
