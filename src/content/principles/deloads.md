## What the evidence shows

Expert coaches reached consensus (in a Delphi study) on a definition of deloading: a period of reduced training stress designed to reduce fatigue, promote recovery and enhance readiness for later training. The study was an expert opinion process, not an outcome trial, so the timing and size of a deload are not established by controlled evidence.

## How this plan uses it

The five-week block on each program page ramps proximity to failure over four weeks and then reduces volume in week five. Treat the exact timing as a starting point and adjust to your own recovery.

## Limits

The frequency, length and design of a deload are practitioner conventions. If you feel fine, there is no strong evidence that you must deload on schedule.
