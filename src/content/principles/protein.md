## What the evidence shows

Meta-analyses of randomised trials found that protein supplementation enhanced gains in muscle mass and strength during resistance training, with benefit levelling off at total intakes of about 1.6 g per kg per day. A second meta-analysis found small additional lean-mass gains in adults under 65 who trained and ate at least that much.

## Limits

The effects are small and individual needs vary. This page is not a nutrition plan. See a dietitian, especially if you have kidney or other medical conditions. A fuller nutrition section is out of scope for now.
