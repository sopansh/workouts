## Two reasonable readings

A 2023 meta-analysis found no evidence that training to momentary failure beats stopping short of failure for hypertrophy. A 2024 meta-regression found hypertrophy improved the closer sets ended to failure, but its reps-in-reserve values were estimated from the studies' descriptions, so it is exploratory. Both can be true: hard sets matter, but failure is not required on every set.

## Reps in reserve (RIR)

Reps in reserve is the number of reps you could still have done when you stop the set. The block plan on each program page ramps RIR across four weeks, then drops volume in a deload.

## Limits

RIR is a self-estimate, so it is a guide rather than a measurement. It is easiest to judge on exercises you know well.
