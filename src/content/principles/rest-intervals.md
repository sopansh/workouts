## What the evidence shows

A Bayesian meta-analysis found a small hypertrophy benefit to resting longer than 60 seconds, and no appreciable difference beyond about 90 seconds. A trial in 21 resistance-trained men found three minutes beat one minute for quadriceps thickness and strength.

## Why heavy lifts need more

The benefit of longer rests is probably mediated by being able to do more work on later sets. That matters most on heavy compound lifts and least on small isolation exercises.

## Limits

The effect is small and results are heterogeneous. Time is also a constraint: this plan uses the time budget on a 90-minute session.
