## Status of this page

These are plan conventions (practitioner practice), not graded evidence. The evidence on how best to progress load in trained lifters is limited, and the page will be revised as sources are added.

## Why track it

Progressive overload is only useful if it is measured. Log load, reps and reps in reserve for every working set. A rising trend on the main lifts over weeks is the simplest sign that the plan is working.
