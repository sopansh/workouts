Every exercise

Each exercise lists what it works, how to set up and perform it, the common mistakes, who should not do it, and an easier regression. Start every jump on the floor.

Pain rule for every exercise

Stay at 2/10 discomfort or lower, and no worse the next morning. A dull muscle effort in the calves is expected. Sharp, pinching or catching pain in the back, hip, knee, ankle, foot or Achilles means stop the set and use the regression. Do not push through it.

See a clinician for a sudden pop or tearing sensation, a visible deformity, numbness or tingling, pain that is worse at night, or pain not improving after 2 to 3 weeks.
