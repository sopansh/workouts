Weekly plan

Two sessions, repeated

Session A is vertical power and Session B is lateral control. Both share the same warm-up, calf work and balance work. Leave at least 48 hours between them.

An example week

Any two days at least 48 hours apart will do. This example uses Wednesday and Friday, which are rest days in the paired strength split.

    The sessions

    Rest roughly 60 to 90 seconds between jump sets, longer if you need it. Stop a set when landings become noisy, uncontrolled or painful. Doses below are the starting point for weeks 1 and 2; the progression page shows how they change. Each exercise links to its full instructions.

    Time

    The warm-up takes about 8 minutes. The jump and calf work follows, with the rest between sets, and fits comfortably in 30 to 40 minutes at the starting doses. This is an estimate from the listed sets and rests, not a timed measurement.
