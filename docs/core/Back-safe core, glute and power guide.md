# Back-safe core, glute and power guide

Draft exercise reference for healthy adults, not an individual rehabilitation or return-to-sport plan. With a current injury or medical condition, ask a qualified clinician before using the guide. The 3D figures are approximate movement references, not anatomical measurements or form checks.

## Exercise stages

These are coaching conventions, not recovery milestones or a prescribed timetable. The rendered page uses `src/content/core/phases.yml`; refer there for the current stage descriptions.

| Phase | Move on when | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Stabilise** | Use the exercise instructions and an appropriate regression | Choose a small selection of holds and controlled movements, with rest between sessions. |
| **2. Load** | Controlled movements feel manageable without pain during the session or worsening symptoms afterwards | Add goblet squat, then raised kettlebell deadlifts and carries. |
| **3. Power** | Phase 2 loads feel easy and pain-free | Kettlebell swings, then low-volume squat and broad jumps. |
